Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026 • Effective for all members and visitors

🇳🇬 Nigeria 🇬🇧 UK 🇨🇦 Canada

Your privacy is the foundation of the Creator Co‑Op loop. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform. We are committed to total transparency, data minimisation, and strict adherence to Nigerian data protection regulations as well as global best practices.

1. Information We Collect• 2. How We Use Your Info• 3. Sharing & Disclosure• 4. Data Security & Cloaking• 5. Your Rights• 6. Cookies & Tracking• 7. Changes to Policy

database 1. Information We Collect

To operate the closed‑loop cooperative and maintain security, we collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account Information: Full name (or username), email address, encrypted password, and social media profile URL (mandatory for verification — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, Telegram, or LinkedIn).
  • Engagement Data: Records of posts submitted to the loop, assigned hourly slots, engagement history (likes, comments, shares traded), and timestamps to enforce N-1 mathematical balance.
  • Payment & Billing Data: Token fee payments processed via secure third‑party gateways (we do not store full card details; only transaction confirmation and date).
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and referrer headers (partially stripped via cloaking), collected for security and performance monitoring.
  • Support Communications: Any messages, feedback, or disputes submitted to our help centre.

We never collect sensitive data (e.g., financial passwords, private messages from social platforms, or location tracking). All data is minimised to what is strictly necessary for the cooperative function.

settings 2. How We Use Your Information

Your data powers the zero‑waste Creator Loop and is used exclusively for:

  • Managing your membership, login, and dashboard access.
  • Assigning hourly slots and distributing engagement tasks fairly (N-1 logic).
  • Verifying social media profile authenticity to prevent bots and duplicate accounts.
  • Calculating engagement credits and ensuring reciprocity among members.
  • Communicating important service updates, loop status, and policy changes.
  • Improving platform security, detecting fraudulent activity, and enforcing our Terms of Service.
  • Complying with legal obligations and responding to lawful requests from authorities (only when required by Nigerian law).

We never sell your personal data to advertisers, data brokers, or third‑party platforms. The Creator Co‑Op is a closed cooperative, not an ad‑driven business.

share 3. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not share your personal information with external parties except in the following limited circumstances:

  • Within the Cooperative: Your social media profile link and username are visible to other members to enable engagement. However, your email address and payment details remain private.
  • Service Providers: We use trusted third‑party services for payment processing (e.g., Paystack or Flutterwave), cloud hosting (e.g., AWS or DigitalOcean), and error tracking. These providers are contractually bound to handle data only for the purposes we specify.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by a valid court order, subpoena, or Nigerian data protection law (NDPR), we may disclose limited information after reviewing the request's legality.
  • Business Transfers: In the unlikely event of a merger or acquisition, member data would be transferred under the same privacy commitments.
gpp_maybe We never share individual engagement statistics with external platforms, and our referrer‑cloaking technology ensures that social media analytics never see our domain as the traffic source.

security 4. Data Security & Referrer Cloaking

Security is engineered into the loop:

  • All passwords are hashed using bcrypt; we never store plaintext passwords.
  • Communications between your browser and our servers are encrypted via TLS 1.3 (HTTPS).
  • Our PWA implements strict Referrer-Policy: no-referrer and custom cloaking headers. When you click a peer's content link, the outgoing HTTP referrer is completely stripped — ensuring that all social media platforms register the visit as "Direct Traffic", not as coming from the Co‑Op domain.
  • Access to internal systems is restricted to essential personnel only, with multi‑factor authentication.
  • Automated scripts, bots, and fake engagement tools are actively blocked via behavioral heuristics.

However, no online platform is 100% secure. We encourage members to use strong unique passwords and to never share their login credentials.

assignment_ind 5. Your Rights & Choices

As a member of the Creator Co‑Op (or a visitor), you have the following rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and similar frameworks:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Update or correct inaccurate information (e.g., email, social media links).
  • Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your account and associated data, subject to any legal retention obligations (e.g., transaction records for 5 years).
  • Restriction: Temporarily limit how we process your data while a dispute is resolved.
  • Data Portability: Receive your engagement history and account data in a structured, machine‑readable format.
  • Withdraw Consent: You may cancel your membership at any time; this will stop all data processing except for mandatory retention.

To exercise any right, please contact our Data Protection Officer at legal@creatorcooptechnologies.com. We will respond within 30 days.

cookie 6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use only essential cookies to operate the dashboard and maintain session security. No advertising or cross‑site tracking cookies are deployed. Specifically:

  • Session cookie: Stores your login state (expires on logout).
  • Preference cookie: Remembers your dashboard layout or language preference (if any).
  • Security token: Used to protect against CSRF attacks.

You may disable cookies in your browser, but certain features (e.g., staying logged in) may not function properly. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third‑party analytics that could leak your activity to external advertisers.

schedule 7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data as long as your membership is active. After account deletion (or membership expiration):

  • Account credentials and personal identifiers are deleted within 30 days.
  • Anonymised engagement logs (e.g., "slot X received Y interactions") may be kept for statistical and research purposes, with all personal identifiers removed.
  • Financial transaction records are retained for 5 years to comply with tax and anti‑fraud regulations.

family_history 8. Children's Privacy

The Creator Co‑Op is strictly for individuals aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we become aware that a member is under 18, we will terminate the account and delete related information.

public 9. International Data Transfers

Our servers are primarily located in Nigeria and the European Union (for redundancy). By using the platform, you consent to data processing in these regions. We ensure that any data transfer complies with NDPR adequacy requirements. The Creator Co‑Op serves Nigerians in Nigeria, the United Kingdom, and Canada, and we are committed to protecting your data regardless of where you access the platform.

update 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect operational changes, legal requirements, or new features. The "Last updated" date at the top will change accordingly. If we make material changes, we will notify active members via email or dashboard announcement.
