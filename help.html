quiz Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Creator Co-Op and how does it work? expand_more

The Creator Co-Op is a private, closed-loop engagement network for Nigerians in Nigeria, the UK, and Canada. Members submit their social media posts (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, Telegram, LinkedIn, and more), are assigned hourly slots, and must engage with peers' content. Each post receives exactly (N-1) engagements (N = active members), triggering organic algorithm boosts. No bots, no fake engagement — just real people helping each other.

Is this a bot or spam network? expand_more

No. Creator Co-Op uses real people engaging with each other's content. No bots, no scripts, no automation. Every member is a verified Nigerian in Nigeria, the UK, or Canada with "skin in the game" through paid membership.

Which social media platforms are supported? expand_more

The Creator Co-Op works with all major social media platforms including Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X (Twitter), YouTube, Telegram, LinkedIn, and more. You must have an active account on the platform you're submitting content for.

How much does membership cost? expand_more

Annual membership is ₦5,000. Founding members receive a 50% lifetime discount at ₦2,500/year (limited to first 50 members).

How much time do I need to commit? expand_more

15 minutes per day during your assigned hourly slot.

What if I miss my assigned slot? expand_more

Missing your slot results in a strike. Three strikes within 30 days lead to temporary suspension.

Can I submit multiple posts per day? expand_more

No. One post per 24 hours across all platforms.

What is the No Freeloader Policy? expand_more

New members must subscribe within 7 days of sign-up. Failure to subscribe within 7 days results in automatic account deactivation.

What if my social media account gets shadowbanned? expand_more

Creator Co-Op uses referrer cloaking to protect your account. All platforms see engagement as organic direct traffic. We continually update our cloaking methods to stay ahead of platform changes.

Who can join Creator Co-Op? expand_more

Anyone who is a Nigerian citizen or resident living in Nigeria, the United Kingdom, or Canada. This includes content creators, influencers, digital publishers, small businesses (SMEs), and anyone who posts content on social media.

task_alt

How to complete your slot efficiently

  • Log in 5 minutes before your slot begins.
  • Open each engagement link in a new tab (right‑click > open link).
  • Spend at least 15–30 seconds on each post; scroll, like, and leave a genuine comment.
  • Do not close tabs immediately — mimic organic behaviour.
  • After engaging, click "Mark as Completed" on your dashboard for each task.
warning

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using the same comment repeatedly (e.g., "Nice post!" on every link).
  • Clicking links but not scrolling or engaging meaningfully.
  • Attempting to use browser automation or macros.
  • Sharing your dashboard password with others.
  • Submitting posts that violate any platform's guidelines (spam, offensive content).
