1. Introduction

1.1 What is Creator Co-Op?

Creator Co-Op is a zero-waste, peer-to-peer organic engagement network for Nigerians in Nigeria, the United Kingdom, and Canada.

It is a private, technology-driven mutual collaboration network built exclusively for content creators, influencers, digital publishers, small businesses (SMEs), and anyone who posts content on any social media platform.

"We are all creators/publishers, and we are all engagers."

Every member holds a dual responsibility: to create content and to engage with others' content.

2. How the Cooperative Works

2.1 The N-1 Mathematical Guarantee

N = Total number of active members in the cooperative

Every post submitted receives exactly (N-1) engagements before it expires.

  • If there are 100 active members → each post gets 99 engagements
  • If there are 200 active members → each post gets 199 engagements
Why N-1?

Because you do not engage with your own post. Everyone else does.

BenefitExplanation
Perfect ReciprocityEvery member's content gets the same support
No FreeloadersEvery member must engage to receive engagement
No FavoritesThe system is mathematically fair to all
Zero WasteEvery engagement goes to a post that still needs it

2.2 The Engagement Loop

Flow Diagram

User submits social media post URL (1 per 24 hours)
    │
    ▼
Post enters boost_links table (status: active)
    │
    ▼
Other members in their assigned hourly slot see the post
    │
    ▼
Each member opens link, engages (like/comment), then clicks "Mark Engaged"
    │
    ▼
System records completed_engagements + increments clicks_received
    │
    ▼
When clicks_received reaches (N-1) where N = total active members
    │
    ▼
Post expires → removed from other users' feeds

The Hourly Slot System

There are 24 hours in a day. Members are distributed evenly across the hours. If you are assigned the 7 AM slot:

  1. Log into the dashboard between 7 AM and 8 AM
  2. Open each engagement link
  3. Watch the content (at least 15 seconds)
  4. Leave a genuine comment
  5. Click "Mark as Engaged"

Your daily responsibility takes about 15 minutes.

5. Getting Started

5.1 Creating Your Social Media Accounts

The Creator Co-Op works with multiple social media platforms. Here's how to create accounts on each:

Facebook

  1. Go to facebook.com
  2. Click "Create New Account"
  3. Enter your name, email address, password, date of birth, and gender
  4. Click "Sign Up"
  5. Check your email for a verification code and enter it

Instagram

  1. Download the Instagram app or go to instagram.com
  2. Tap "Sign up" and enter your email or phone number
  3. Create a username and password
  4. Fill in your profile info

TikTok

  1. Download the TikTok app
  2. Tap "Sign up" and choose your method (email or phone)
  3. Create a username and password
  4. Set up your profile

X (Twitter)

  1. Go to x.com or download the app
  2. Click "Sign up"
  3. Enter your name, email, and create a password
  4. Choose a username

YouTube

  1. Go to youtube.com
  2. Sign in with your Google account
  3. Click your profile picture → "Create a channel"
  4. Enter your channel name and details

Telegram

  1. Download Telegram from your app store
  2. Enter your phone number to sign up
  3. Create a profile with your name and photo

LinkedIn

  1. Go to linkedin.com
  2. Click "Join now"
  3. Enter your name, email, and password
  4. Complete your profile

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is the Creator Co-Op and how does it work?

A private, closed-loop engagement network for Nigerians in Nigeria, the UK, and Canada. Members submit posts from any social media platform, are assigned hourly slots, and must engage with peers. Each post receives (N-1) engagements.

Q2: Is this a bot or spam network?

No. Creator Co-Op uses real people engaging with each other's content. No bots, no scripts, no automation.

Q3: How much does membership cost?

Monthly membership is ₦200. One boost per 24 hours (30 boosts per month).

Q4: How much time do I need to commit?

15 minutes per day during your assigned hourly slot.

Q5: What if I miss my assigned slot?

Missing your slot results in a strike. Three strikes within 30 days lead to temporary suspension.

Q6: Can I submit multiple posts per day?

No. One post per 24 hours across any platform.

Q7: What is the No Freeloader Policy?

New members must subscribe within 7 days of sign-up. Failure to subscribe within 7 days results in automatic account deactivation.

Q8: What if my content gets shadowbanned?

Creator Co-Op uses referrer cloaking to protect your content across all platforms. Platforms see engagement as organic direct traffic.

Q9: Which platforms are supported?

Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, Telegram, LinkedIn, and more. You must have an active account on the platform you're submitting content for.

Q10: Who can join?

Any Nigerian citizen or resident living in Nigeria, the United Kingdom, or Canada. This includes content creators, influencers, digital publishers, small businesses (SMEs), and anyone who posts content on social media.
